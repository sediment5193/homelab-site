This is the first post. Use it to explain what this site is for, what your
lab looks like right now, and what you're planning to document going
forward.

A few ideas for what to write about as you go:

- Setup/teardown of a new service (what problem it solves, how you deployed it)
- Networking changes (VLANs, firewall rules, DNS)
- Outages and how you diagnosed/fixed them — these make great portfolio content
- Automation you wrote (Ansible playbooks, scripts, CI/CD for your lab)
- Hardware changes or upgrades

Delete this post once you've got real content, or keep it around and edit
it into a proper "start here" post.
